隐私政策

生效及更新日期：2026 年 10 月 24 日
适用于：日子有账（Daily Ledger）iOS App 及本说明网站

我们是「日子有账」的开发者与维护者。本政策说明你使用 App 时，信息如何被处理，以及你可以如何管理这些信息。隐私相关问题请联系 xuehui1004@gmail.com。

本地优先，由你决定。

基础记账无需注册 App 自有账号。我们不设开发者账本服务器，不出售个人信息，不使用广告追踪或跨 App 追踪。账本默认保存在设备上；可选 iCloud、导出、分享与邮件支持会按你的主动操作处理相应信息。

处理的信息iCloud 与购买系统权限保留与删除English policy

1. 处理哪些信息，为什么处理

  • 你主动录入或导入的内容：消费金额、日期、币种、分类、备注、退款、物品档案与封面照片、购入和使用记录、手动估值、分摊活动、成员称呼与结算记录，用于记账、统计、物品追踪和分摊计算。这些内容默认在本机处理和保存。
  • 本机设置与操作状态：语言偏好、导入记录及使用额度、提醒操作状态等，用于保存设置、避免重复操作和维持功能。语言及评分提示计数等轻量偏好保存在系统偏好存储中。
  • 本机安全设置：如你启用账单密码，带随机盐的密码派生摘要及保护状态保存在设备专属 Keychain 中，不随业务备份或手动 iCloud 账本同步传输。App 锁和密码找回通过系统身份验证完成；App 不获取 Face ID、Touch ID 的生物特征模板或设备密码。
  • 你主动发送的支持信息：通过电子邮件联系我们时，你的邮箱、邮件内容及你选择附加的截图等由邮件服务处理，并由我们用于答复、排查问题和处理隐私请求。请勿发送密码、支付卡信息或未经遮挡的完整账本。

当前 App 不接入第三方广告、行为分析或第三方崩溃上报服务，也不因记账而读取通讯录、定位、麦克风或相机。

2. 可选 iCloud 与 App Store 购买

iCloud：仅在功能可用且你主动操作、预览并确认后，账本快照（包括物品封面、关联记录及尚在回收站中的内容）才会写入你个人 Apple 账号的 CloudKit 私有数据库。后续修改需要再次手动同步。App 不把这些记录上传到开发者服务器，我们不通过该 App 读取你的私有云端账本。Apple 会按其服务规则处理账号、云存储和必要技术信息。网络、签名配置、账号或空间问题可能使同步不可用；操作结果以 App 实际提示为准。

购买：如提供 Pro 购买或恢复购买，由 Apple App Store 处理支付，App 通过 StoreKit 验证交易和权益。App 不获取你的支付卡资料。购买账号和 iCloud 数据账号分别管理；恢复购买不会恢复或替换账本。商品与购买可用性以 App Store 和 App 内实际状态为准。

Apple 的相关信息见 Apple 隐私政策。Apple 服务的数据存储地区和跨境处理由你的账号地区及 Apple 适用规则决定。

3. 系统权限与你的选择

  • 照片选择：通过系统照片选择器，仅接收你选中的图片，并在本机压缩为物品封面；不申请整个相册读取权限。
  • 保存图片：仅在你确认保存预览图片时申请相册添加权限。保存后的图片由系统照片图库管理，并可能随你自己的 iCloud 照片设置同步。
  • 日历：仅为你确认的分摊收款提醒请求权限。iOS 17 及以上使用仅写入权限，较旧系统使用系统提供的日历事件权限；功能不用于扫描其他日历事件。默认提醒只含通用标题和时间，你主动打开「包含收款详情」后，事件备注才含活动名和当前待收金额。事件可能随目标日历同步到其他设备或向该日历的其他可见者展示。
  • 身份验证：按你的操作调用系统 Face ID、Touch ID 或设备密码验证，用于 App 锁和本机账单密码恢复。
  • 文件：通过系统文件选择器读取你选定的 CSV 或备份，或将你确认的导出文件保存到所选位置。

你可以在 iOS「设置」中管理权限。拒绝某项权限只会影响依赖它的相应功能，基础手动记账仍可使用。

4. 导出、分享与数据安全

账本和封面保存在 App 本机目录并使用 iOS 文件保护。账单密码保护个人账单的界面读取，不代表物品、分摊、导出文件或备份都受同一密码保护。导出的 CSV、JSON 完整备份及分享图片可能包含私人信息，业务备份没有额外的密码加密。

导出或分享由你确认，目标文件服务、邮件服务或其他 App 按各自的隐私政策处理内容。请仅向可信位置保存和分享，并自行保护导出副本。系统设备备份可能依据你的 iOS / iCloud 设置包含 App 数据，这与 App 内主动手动同步分别管理。

App 不自动向分摊成员发送账单、提醒或消息；分享图片和日历提醒均由你主动发起。

5. 保存期限、删除与其他权利

本机记录保留至你删除或移除 App。进入回收站的记录通常保留 30 天，由 App 运行时清理过期项目；你也可以在回收站永久删除。合并或恢复前生成的本机安全副本是独立副本，删除当前账本记录不会自动清除旧安全副本。

如曾使用 iCloud，同步中的删除需要你再次明确同步；旧云端快照不会因本机卸载自动消失。如需移除云端数据，可在 iOS 的 Apple 账号 → iCloud → 存储管理中查找本 App 数据并按系统提供的选项处理；系统版本、显示名称及可用选项可能不同，可联系支持获取指引。

卸载 App 通常会移除本机 App 容器内的账本和安全副本，但不会自动删除已导出的文件、相册图片、日历事件、系统备份或云端数据；这些副本需在各自位置管理。设备 Keychain 中的本机保护材料也可能在卸载后保留；如需清除，请在卸载前于 App 内关闭账单密码及 App 锁。卸载前请先备份需要保留的数据。

你可以在 App 内查看、更正、导出或删除记录。开发者无法替你读取、恢复或远程删除仅存于你设备或私有 iCloud 的账本。支持邮件仅为处理你的请求及必要后续沟通而保留；如需访问、更正或删除你发送给我们的支持信息，请通过本页邮箱联系。依法必须保留的信息除外。

6. 儿童、政策更新与联系

本 App 面向一般用户，不以未满 13 周岁（或当地法律规定的更高年龄）的儿童为目标，也不会有意通过支持渠道收集其个人信息。若你认为儿童向我们发送了个人信息，请联系我们处理。

功能或数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本页与日期；涉及需要额外授权的处理，会在相应操作前说明并按适用法律取得授权。

Privacy Policy — Daily Ledger

Effective / last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy applies to the Daily Ledger iOS app, also known as 日子有账, and this information website. The developer and maintainer can be reached at xuehui1004@gmail.com (GitHub: Hxxxxx).

Information and purpose

Basic bookkeeping requires no app account. Expenses, refunds, categories, dates, notes, item photos, item costs, manual valuations, usage records, shared-expense member names and settlements are processed and stored on your device by default. Preferences, import history and operation status support settings, quotas and duplicate prevention. We do not run a developer ledger server, sell personal information, or use advertising, cross-app tracking, third-party behavioral analytics or third-party crash reporting.

Optional Apple services

When available, manual iCloud sync sends a ledger snapshot, including item covers and records still in the recycle bin, to your private CloudKit database only after your explicit action and confirmation. Further edits require another manual sync. We do not use the app to access your private cloud ledger. Apple processes the account, storage and technical information required for its services under its Privacy Policy, including applicable storage locations and international transfers. Sync may be unavailable depending on configuration, account, network and storage conditions.

If Pro purchases are available, Apple handles payment. StoreKit provides verified transaction and entitlement information to the app; we do not receive payment card details. Purchases and iCloud ledger data are managed separately. Prices and availability are shown in the app and App Store.

Permissions and security

The system photo picker supplies only images you select, which are resized locally for item covers. Saving a preview image requests add-only photo access. Calendar access is requested only for reminders you confirm: write-only on iOS 17 or later, and event access on older systems. The app does not scan unrelated calendar events. Reminders use a generic title and time by default; enabling details adds the activity name and current receivable amount to the notes. Your calendar or photo service may sync saved content according to your settings.

System authentication is used for app locking or passcode recovery; the app does not receive biometric templates or your device passcode. A salted bill-passcode verifier and protection state are stored in device-only Keychain and excluded from ledger backups and manual ledger sync. Local ledger files use iOS file protection. The bill passcode controls personal-bill screens, and does not encrypt exported files or protect every item and shared-expense screen.

Exports, retention and control

You choose files to import and confirm exports or sharing through system interfaces. CSV, JSON backups and shared images may contain private information; exported backups have no additional password encryption. Destination providers apply their own policies. iOS device backups may also include app data according to your settings. The app does not automatically message shared-expense members.

You can review, correct, export and delete records in the app. Recycle-bin records normally remain for 30 days and are cleaned up when the app runs, or you may permanently delete them earlier. Safety snapshots created before merges or restores are separate local copies and are not removed by deleting a current record. Cloud deletions require explicit sync, and older cloud snapshots are not removed by uninstalling. To remove cloud storage, check the app under Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage; options vary by system version.

Uninstalling normally removes the app container, including local records and safety copies. It does not automatically delete exported files, saved photos, calendar events, device backups or iCloud data; manage these at their respective locations. Device Keychain protection material may survive uninstalling. To clear that material, disable the bill passcode and app lock in the app before uninstalling. Export anything you want to keep first. We cannot remotely access, restore or delete records held only on your device or in your private iCloud database.

Support, children and updates

When you email us, your email address, message and chosen attachments are handled by the email provider and by us to respond and resolve your request. Avoid sending passwords, payment card details or unredacted ledgers. We retain support messages for handling your request and necessary follow-up; contact us to request access, correction or deletion, subject to legal retention duties. The app is not directed to children under 13 or a higher local minimum age, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information through support. Contact us if a child has sent such information.

We update this page and its date when practices change, and explain and request any additional authorization where required. For privacy requests, email xuehui1004@gmail.com.
